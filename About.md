# About Broke Barista

**Broke Barista** is a personal coffee tracking web app built and maintained by [Gaurav Powar](https://github.com/GauravPowar).

## Why this exists

The home espresso rabbit hole is expensive. After picking up a Dedica Duo and a hand grinder, it became clear that tracking purchases, dials, and tasting notes in a Notes app wasn't cutting it. Broke Barista is the answer — a purpose-built tool for a single user, optimised for daily friction-free logging.

The name is honest: great coffee at home costs real money upfront, but the savings vs café prices add up fast. This app keeps score.

## What it tracks

- **Every purchase** — beans, gear, accessories, combo orders with cost splitting
- **Every brew** — full recipe (dose, yield, time, temp, grind), rating, tasting notes, milk-based flag
- **The shelf** — what bag is open, how many grams are left, rest days, roast timeline
- **Monthly stories** — editorial recaps with charts, highlights, and flavor palette evolution

## Stack

Zero-dependency, zero-build-step web app:

- **Frontend** — Vanilla HTML, CSS, JS + Chart.js
- **Backend** — Cloudflare Pages Functions (TypeScript)
- **Database** — Cloudflare D1 (SQLite at the edge)
- **Hosting** — Cloudflare Pages (free tier)
